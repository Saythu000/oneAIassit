### Slide 2: The $250B Industry Crisis
* **Slide Title:** The Insurance Conversion & Servicing Void
* **Subtitle:** Why Traditional Chatbots & Human Call Centers Are Costing Agencies Billions
* **Key Visuals / Layout:**
  * 3 Problem Column Cards with red alert accent styling.
  * Callout statistic banner: *"67% of inbound insurance leads drop off during intake; 42% of customer service costs are spent on repetitive billing queries."*
* **Core Pain Points:**
  1. **High Lead Drop-Off & Clunky Intake (CAC Loss):**
     * Traditional forms and rigid tree-bots frustrate users. When prospects ask simple brochure questions mid-intake (e.g., *"Do you cover maternity?"*), generic bots break or restart from scratch.
  2. **LLM Hallucination & Financial Liability Risk:**
     * Generic chatbots invent premium rates, coverage limits, and payment dates—creating massive legal and regulatory liability for insurance brokers.
  3. **High Servicing Overhead & Off-Hours Inactivity:**
     * Over 60% of policyholders message after business hours. Human call centers are costly ($6–$12 per call), slow to answer due date queries, and incapable of instant scaling.
  4. **Regulatory Risk & Compliance Blindspots:**
     * Regulators (State DOIs) heavily penalize mishandled cancellation requests, consumer grievances, and legal disputes. Traditional bots cannot distinguish routine questions from compliance crises.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Insurance brokerage has a massive structural problem: acquiring a lead costs up to $150, but over two-thirds drop off during the intake process because the tools are rigid and unresponsive. Worse, agencies testing standard generative AI run into hallucinations—bots inventing policy terms that brokers legally cannot honor. Meanwhile, customer service desks are overwhelmed with routine questions like 'When is my next premium due?'. The industry desperately needs a solution that combines the conversational empathy of modern AI with the strict deterministic accuracy of enterprise financial software."*

### Slide 5: Deep Dive — The AI Sales Agent
* **Slide Title:** Product Pillar 1: The Autonomous Sales Agent
* **Subtitle:** Dynamic Intake, Interruption Resilience & 0%-Hallucination Policy Matching
* **Key Visuals / Layout:**
  * Interactive WhatsApp conversation mockup showcasing turn-by-turn question progression, mid-flow brochure question answering, and final policy recommendation card.
* **Feature Highlights:**
  * **Dynamic Database-Driven Intake:**
    * Questions, sequence order, and validation types (`US_STATE`, `CURRENCY`, `NUMBER`, `ENUM`) are configured directly from the admin studio without code deployments.
  * **Brochure RAG with Context Resumption Anchors:**
    * If a prospect asks *"Do you cover diabetes?"* during step 3, the agent performs semantic RAG, delivers a concise 2-sentence answer, and immediately re-prompts step 3 (*"Now, what is your maximum monthly budget?"*).
  * **3-Turn Interruption Guard:**
    * Automatically prevents conversational loops by nudging users back to complete their quote after 3 consecutive tangents.
  * **2-Stage Grounded Recommendation Engine (Strict 0/1/>1 Branching):**
    * *0 Matches:* Bypasses LLM $\rightarrow$ flags `HUMAN_ESCALATED` for human specialist outreach.
    * *1 Match:* Bypasses LLM ranking $\rightarrow$ formats direct single policy pitch with zero latency.
    * *>1 Matches:* Grounded synthesis strictly restricted to retrieved candidate IDs.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Let's look at what makes our Sales Agent unique. Unlike typical chatbots that break when a user deviates, our engine uses validation-first parsing and brochure RAG with resumption anchors. If a prospect asks about dental coverage halfway through onboarding, the agent answers accurately and immediately re-anchors the conversation back to the next question. When intake finishes, our 2-Stage Recommendation Engine queries the database deterministically—guaranteeing that every policy price, deductible, and benefit pitched to the customer is 100% grounded in real catalog data."*

### Slide 7: Enterprise Shield & Compliance
* **Slide Title:** Enterprise Shield: Fail-Closed Compliance & Multi-Tenant Security
* **Subtitle:** Protecting Insurance Brokerages from Regulatory Penalties & Data Breaches
* **Key Visuals / Layout:**
  * Visual shield diagram with 4 safety rings: Deterministic Compliance Gate $\rightarrow$ Audit Log $\rightarrow$ PostgreSQL Tenant RLS $\rightarrow$ E.164 Identity Resolver.
* **Safety Pillars:**
  1. **Fail-Closed Hard Compliance Gate (<5ms SLA):**
     * Upstream regex engine deterministically intercepts legal threats (*"attorney", "lawsuit"*), regulatory DOI complaints (*"insurance commissioner"*), fraud grievances, and cancellation requests before any LLM is called.
  2. **Automated Supervisor Alerts & Audit Trail:**
     * Triggers immediate high-priority supervisor alerts and writes permanent entries to the `AuditLog` table.
  3. **PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS):**
     * Database session variables (`app.current_tenant_id`) enforce strict kernel-level tenant isolation, guaranteeing zero cross-agency data exposure.
  4. **Idempotent Dispatch & E.164 Normalization:**
     * Normalizes international phone formats and eliminates duplicate message sending via `clientMessageId` tracking.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"In the insurance sector, compliance is non-negotiable. If a consumer mentions filing a complaint with the state insurance commissioner or hiring a lawyer, an AI bot should never attempt to argue. Our Fail-Closed Hard Compliance Gate operates upstream in under 5 milliseconds—instantly freezing automated replies, issuing a compliant holding message, logging the incident to the audit trail, and paging a human supervisor immediately."*

### Slide 8: Technical Architecture & Reliability
* **Slide Title:** High-Throughput Cloud Architecture
* **Subtitle:** Built for Sub-50ms Ingress, Zero-Drop Concurrency & High Availability
* **Key Visuals / Layout:**
  * Technical block diagram: Omnichannel Ingress $\rightarrow$ Sub-50ms Atomic Queue $\rightarrow$ Lane Mutex $\rightarrow$ Dual Agents $\rightarrow$ PostgreSQL RLS & Socket.io.
* **Architecture Pillars:**
  * **Sub-50ms Atomic Ingress:** `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT ("tenantId", "wamId") DO NOTHING` acknowledges Meta Cloud webhooks in $<50\text{ ms}$, preventing webhook timeouts and message drops.
  * **Per-Customer FIFO Lane Mutex:** In-memory queue ensures rapid back-to-back user messages are processed in strict sequential order with zero out-of-order state corruption.
  * **5-Minute Stale-Lock Supervisor:** Automatically recovers stuck jobs and routes terminal failures to the dead-letter queue after 3 retries.
  * **Real-Time WebSocket Layer:** Socket.io gateway (:3001) streams live chat messages and qualification state changes to the admin console instantly.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Under the hood, OneAI Assist is built with mission-critical reliability. Our ingress layer acknowledges webhooks in under 50 milliseconds using atomic PostgreSQL upserts, ensuring Meta Cloud API never drops a message. Our per-customer FIFO Lane Mutex prevents race conditions when users send rapid messages, and our stale-lock supervisor automatically heals transient failures."*
